💡 Engineering Sizing Note for Customers:
• For clean tap water, max allowable watt density is 8–10 W/cm² (50–65 W/in²).
• If hard water or mineral scaling is expected, reduce watt density to 5–6 W/cm² to prevent scale crust bake-on and tube burnout.
• For thermal oil heating, keep watt density ≤ 2.5–3.0 W/cm² to avoid oil cracking and carbon deposits.
